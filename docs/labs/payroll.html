What the model assumes

Federal brackets are simplified, rounded, and illustrative. They are not a filing engine.
State tax is a flat rate applied to gross pay minus pre-tax retirement. Real states use their own bases and credits.
Social Security uses a wage base; Medicare is applied to all wages. Local taxes, FSA/HSA, and bonuses are not modeled.
For education only. Do not use this output for tax filing or payroll compliance.
